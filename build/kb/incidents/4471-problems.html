How each report was placed

  • Read the body, not the subject. Subjects were picked from the same handful of phrases for every problem, so they cannot split one.
  • Tenant, not browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts, locales.ts). Intake’s tag guesses from the reporter’s browser, and it is wrong for dozens of these reports.
  • A flipped month is not a one-day move. The desk already listed five reports that read as one day off (desk-notes.json). The #4471 page still counts them under parser or hint by tenant. No swap of day and month gives one day off, and one report shows the same meeting on two days in two time zones. They are their own problem.
  • The desk’s own count. CauseTable below keeps the five one-day moves under parser, so the desk counts 379 there; the parser ticket carries that count.
  • One id, one report. The export repeats some reports across pages; each counts once.
